Word to PDF

Converts a .docx document to a PDF with selectable text: headings, paragraphs, bold and italic, lists, tables and images.

Older .doc files are not supported; open them in Word or LibreOffice and save as .docx first. Fonts are mapped to standard PDF fonts, so letters outside Western European alphabets are replaced with “?”.

PDF to Word

Turns a PDF into an editable .docx. Text is rebuilt into paragraphs with sizes, bold and italic; scanned pages are kept as images.
